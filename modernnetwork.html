NETWORKING SERVICES

Network access protection, network segmentation, and encrypted communication to protect automation networks against unauthorized access

Network security – for secure industrial communication

Bringing you a unique blend of expertise in both the IT and industrial automation spaces, we provide you with a holistic approach to managing your network infrastructure and security throughout the entirety of its lifecycle.


Global Expertise

Our Networking Services has the ability to provide expertise where you are, with resources strategically located around the globe. Network and Security Services Consultants and Tech Connect support have a broad range of certified personnel on staff.


Assess

Assessments are the starting point of any program implementation. Assessments evaluate your current conditions and highlight any issues you need to address to get you where you want to be. An assessment is the first step to prevent or fix network and security issues. Assessments are performed by evaluating current status, documenting design or implementation issues and offering resolutions, as well as provide information about potential problems that could result in future unplanned downtime or security breaches.


Design

Experience more uptime with a scalable and secure design that aligns with Converged Plantwide Ethernet (CPwE) [PDF]. Modern Networking Services collaborates with you to ensure your network and security systems are built to meet industry standards and your own unique requirements.


Implement

With a design in place, Our Networking Services can help get you up and running quickly. You can rest assured with our thorough processes, you will be spending less time on repairs in the future as your systems will be implemented accurately to perform optimally. Pre-Engineered Packaged Solutions, Turnkey and Custom Solutions are all within our range of capabilities.


Validate

This is your double check. State-of-the-art diagnostic tools are used to conduct installation and operational tests to ensure your specifications are appropriately met and that performance is in compliance with governing standards outlined by ODVA, IEEE, ANSI, TIA, ISA-95, NERC CIP, ISA-99, NIST 800-53 [PDF], NIST 800-82 [PDF]


Monitor & Maintain

Monitor and maintain your network and security infrastructure with ease. One phone number connects you to tailored support – whether you need continuous monitoring or ad hoc diagnostic monitoring, and periodic visits or emergency response. These maintenance services can help improve your network uptime, as well as help you maintain a secure, robust and resilient infrastructure.
